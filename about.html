My Journey

I graduated with a Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Telecommunications, where I developed a strong foundation in engineering principles before transitioning into software development. During this transition, I discovered a strong interest in Data Engineering and began building my fundamentals through structured courses and capstone projects.

To broaden my technical and professional exposure, I pursued further studies in Canada and earned a Postgraduate Diploma in Quality Engineering from Conestoga College. During my time there, I secured an internship at PMA Accelerator, where I worked as a developer on two large-scale client projects led by aspiring project managers. These projects are showcased in my work section.

I also volunteered on an AI GESI-themed, community-driven project, contributing to socially impactful technology initiatives.

Currently, I am seeking professional opportunities in back-end development with a long-term goal of building a career in Data Engineering. To strengthen my backend expertise, I have joined Brototype, where I am specializing in Golang, with the aim of transitioning into Data Engineering and DevOps roles in the future.
